Privacy Policy

Attendance & Salary is an offline attendance and salary tracker.

Data storage

Attendance records, salary profiles and settings are stored locally on the user's device. The app does not require an account and does not send this information to a server.

Sharing and backup

If the user chooses Backup or Share, the app creates a file or invokes the device sharing feature. The user controls where that file is sent.

Permissions

The Android version does not require internet access for its core features.

Contact

For support, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
